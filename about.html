About us

RentCar is a local hire place in town.

We keep a small lot of cars ready to go. That includes small cars, SUVs, vans, utes and a couple of luxury cars like our Subaru WRX.

You book online and we email you to confirm. Then you pick the car up the same day.

Insurance and unlimited kilometres in town come with every hire.

The lot is open every day from 8am to 6pm.
